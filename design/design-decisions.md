# ATITHI — Design Decisions

## 1. Core Product Philosophy

**"The guest is at the centre."**

ATITHI is designed around hospitality rather than software complexity. Every layout and interaction aims to feel:
- **Brand Personality:** Warm, Modern, Intuitive
- **Product Experience:** Simple, Clear, Intuitive

The visual tone intentionally avoids looking like a corporate POS system or an overly playful food-delivery app.

---

## 2. Information Architecture & User Flow

The interface is structured around a linear six-stage guest journey:

`Discover → Choose → Customize → Order → Pay → Track`

Why this structure:
- **Reduces wait friction:** Guests can inspect the menu immediately via table QR without waiting for staff availability.
- **Improves ordering confidence:** Clear item presentation and customization reduce back-and-forth or miscommunication.
- **Creates post-order transparency:** Dedicated checkout confirmation and order tracking remove uncertainty after payment.

---

## 3. Target Audience Considerations

Because restaurant diners include students, working professionals, families, and first-time visitors with varying digital familiarity:
- Navigation and primary actions are kept straightforward with minimal cognitive load.
- Essential food information and pricing remain clear and scannable rather than hidden behind nested menus.
